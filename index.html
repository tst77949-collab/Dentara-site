Questions

Frequently asked

Are digital implants safe compared to traditional ones?

Yes — CBCT guidance means the implant position is planned to a tenth of a millimetre before surgery, giving fewer surprises and faster healing than freehand placement.

Can I really get a crown in one day?

In most cases, yes. The scan, design and milling happen in-house; a permanent milled crown is seated the same afternoon in about four hours.

Do veneers damage my natural teeth?

Ultra-thin no-prep veneers remove almost nothing from the enamel. We only recommend what your simulation shows you need.

What does the AI smile preview cost?

It is included in every consultation. The preview takes one selfie and five minutes, with zero obligation to proceed.

Do you accept insurance and payment plans?

We work with all major digital insurers and offer 6–24 month interest-free plans on implant and veneer packages.
